FAQ

About Deal Ops

What is Deal Ops?

An open-source project of tools for SPV, syndicate and fund operations. Each module does one job. Deal Ops Toolkit reconciles receipts, works out the closing allocation and splits an exit, in your browser. Deal Ops Inbox prepares each email in your operations mailbox for a person to review, on your own server.

Is it free?

Yes. Both modules are open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. The Toolkit needs no sign-up. For the Inbox you pay only for your own server and, if you use one, a hosted model.

Is it a regulated service?

No. Deal Ops is open-source software, not a regulated service. It does not hold money, give advice or act for you. A person checks every result and makes every decision.

Data and trust

Where does my data go?

Toolkit: Your deal data is processed in your browser and never sent by this app. The hosting provider still receives normal page requests. Inbox: it runs on your own server and sends only redacted text to the model provider you configure, or, with a local model, keeps the model traffic inside your network. What is sent to the model

How do the modules fit together?

They are separate tools. Deal Ops Inbox can import the deals, commitments, bank lines and reconciliation ledger you export from the Toolkit as CSV files, read-only, and it suggests actions such as marking an investor withdrawn in the Toolkit. A person makes the change.

How is it tested?

Each module page shows the figures from its latest release checks: the Toolkit and the Inbox. Both compare their rules with an independent Python implementation and use mutation checks. The Inbox is also measured on emails that development never read, with a real model, once per release.
